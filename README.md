## What I'm doing here

This repo is dedicated to my development in ML/AI, and anything else I find interesting along the way. Idealistically, the daily commits should reflect actual progress: notes on books being read, or work on whatever project is active.

Hopefully this becomes a rough roadmap for anyone going from "casually interested in programming/ML" to actually competent and knowledgeable in the field. By "roadmap", I mean what I did to get good.
